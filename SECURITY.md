# Security policy

## Supported versions

QuicSync has not yet published a stable release. Security fixes are applied to
the default branch. Once supported releases exist, this section will list their
support status explicitly.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not disclose a suspected vulnerability in a public issue, discussion, pull
request, or commit.

Report it privately through
[GitHub's private vulnerability reporting form](https://github.com/domMayhew/QuicSync/security/advisories/new).
Include, where possible:

- the affected revision or version;
- impact and likely attack scenario;
- reproduction steps or a proof of concept;
- suggested mitigations; and
- whether or when you intend to disclose the issue.

The maintainers will acknowledge a report within seven calendar days,
investigate it, and coordinate remediation and disclosure with the reporter.
Please allow a reasonable remediation period before public disclosure. If the
private reporting form is unavailable, contact the repository owner through the
contact details on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/domMayhew) and ask for a
private reporting channel without including sensitive details.

Reports made in good faith to protect users are welcome. Avoid accessing data
that is not your own, degrading services, or causing harm while researching an
issue.
